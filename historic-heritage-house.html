HISTORIC HERITAGE HOUSE

Sensitive structural restoration preserving historic Victorian architecture while integrating 21st-century interior comfort and energy retrofits.

Oct 28, 2026

CLIENT
Heritage Trust Properties
ROLE
Restoration Architect
SERVICE
Renovation & Restoration

Work Background

This 19th-century property suffered structural decay.

The objective was repairing compromised timber frames, restoring original brick and wood facades, and modernizing mechanical systems without destroying historical character.

The Outcome

We stabilized the original frame, restored decorative interior moldings, and integrated concealed HVAC and radiant heating networks for complete modern efficiency.
