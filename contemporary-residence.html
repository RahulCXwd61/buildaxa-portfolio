Work Background

Contemporary Residence was designed as a modern family sanctuary emphasizing natural light, open-plan living zones, and seamless outdoor transitions.

The project required balancing privacy from adjacent structures with expansive garden views and active solar shading.

The Outcome

We delivered a light-filled two-story residence featuring structural steel frames, high-performance glass walls, and low-energy thermal insulation.

The open layout seamlessly connects indoor living areas with landscaped patios.
